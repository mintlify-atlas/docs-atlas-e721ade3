In the 2010 original, team modes sat behind a rank gate — weeks of grinding stood between a new player and Capture the Flag. SkillTanki removes that ladder entirely. Every mode — including Assault and Capture the Flag — is open from the first minute, alongside every hull, turret, paint, and map. The game starts with the game.

## The five modes

## Solo vs multiplayer

**Solo vs bots** — an offline battle against computer-controlled tanks. The bot count is adjustable, all the way down to zero if you simply want an empty map to explore. Bots aim with realistic error, reaction delay, and turret traverse — they can be outplayed by manoeuvre just like a live opponent, making solo mode a genuine training ground, not just a warm-up lobby.

**Multiplayer** — a room for live players only; bots are never present in multiplayer rooms. Team modes (Team Deathmatch, Capture the Flag, Assault) are always played with live people, which means every flag run and every co-ordinated push involves real decisions on both sides.

## Public and private rooms

**Open room** — appears in the shared battle list visible to everyone. Any player can browse the list, pick a side, and join. This is how you find random opponents and fill a match quickly.

**Private room** — marked with a lock icon in the battle list. Only players who know the password can enter. The password is never shown in the URL, never displayed in the public list — it travels from you to your friends only. If your connection drops or the page reloads mid-match, you return to your own tank in the same room without losing your score.
