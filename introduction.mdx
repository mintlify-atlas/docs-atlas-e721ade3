SkillTanki is a free browser tank arcade built in the spirit of the 2010 classic — the same angular hulls, the same "box" maps, and that feeling of a heavy machine on suspension. Open the link and a few seconds later you're in battle. No Flash plugin, no account, no download, no progression wall, no wallet advantage. The project exists because the original game accumulated a specific and very familiar set of problems over fifteen years, and SkillTanki is the answer to each one of them.

## Why it was built

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Flash died and took the game with it" icon="plug-circle-xmark">
    In 2020 browsers switched Flash Player off for good, and the classic client had physically nowhere left to run. SkillTanki opens in any modern browser from a plain link — on a home PC, a laptop, or a work machine without admin rights.
  </Card>
  <Card title="That game no longer exists anywhere" icon="clock-rotate-left">
    Fifteen years of updates changed the original beyond recognition: different visuals, different physics, different spirit. There is no official way back. SkillTanki carefully recreates it — the same hulls, the same guns, the same tank behaviour on bumps and ramps.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Community maps were lost for good" icon="map">
    Players spent years building their own maps — the original's updates buried them along with the old format. SkillTanki preserves the classic collection (91 maps), keeps the format alive, and ships a map editor so lost maps can be rebuilt and new ones created from scratch.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Money decided more than hands" icon="coins">
    Supplies were bought with crystals: whoever had the wallet had permanent double damage. SkillTanki has no paid content at all — boosts are infinite and free for everyone, and the balance is tuned around equal players, not around a shop.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Grind before the game" icon="hourglass-half">
    In the original, weeks of farming stood between "I want the Railgun" and "I have the Railgun", and team modes unlocked by rank. In SkillTanki everything — modes, hulls, turrets, paints, maps — is available from the first second. The game starts with the game.
  </Card>
  <Card title="The account as an anchor" icon="lock">
    Registration, e-mail, password, the fear of losing a levelled-up profile if the project shuts down. SkillTanki has nothing to lose and nothing to set up: type a call sign and drive into battle. A ten-minute lunch-break session is a perfectly valid scenario.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Nowhere to play with friends" icon="user-group">
    Matchmaking was a lottery and the classic never offered a room "for our people only". SkillTanki lets you create a private password-protected room in two clicks — your own server for the evening, like LAN parties back in the day.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Unfair wins" icon="shield-halved">
    The outcome of a battle is judged by an impartial server-side referee: damage, speed and hits are verified the same way for everyone, so client-side tricks give no advantage.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## What's included

- **7 hulls** — from the nimble Wasp to the fortress-like Mammoth, all unlocked from the start
- **9 turrets** — Smoky, Ricochet, Railgun, Twins, Thunder, Firebird, Freeze, Isida, and Shaft, each with a distinct firing mechanic
- **101 paints** — pure cosmetics, no armour bonus, all free
- **91 maps** — 53 summer maps, 38 of which have a winter version; the full classic collection preserved
- **5 game modes** — Deathmatch, Team Deathmatch, Capture the Flag, Assault, and Explore Map
- **PWA** — installs directly from the browser on mobile, no app store required
- **Gamepad support** — any standard controller works in the mobile PWA, including the Steam Deck

## How it differs

<Accordion title="No accounts">
  There is no account system, and that is a deliberate design decision, not an unfinished feature. No e-mails, passwords, confirmations, or recoveries. You type a call sign — any name you want to be known by in battle — pick a tank, and drive off to fight. Every mode, hull, turret, paint, and map is open from the first second. Nothing to lose, nothing to set up.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="No pay-to-win">
  There is no paid content whatsoever. All five supplies — Repair kit, Double armour, Double damage, Nitro, and Nuclear energy — are infinite and free for every player. The only limiter is the cooldown after use, identical for everyone. Supplies stopped being merchandise and became tactics: the question is no longer "did you buy it" but "did you press it in time".
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Skill-based balance">
  Since everyone is equal in equipment, the only way to win is by playing better. Every gun's damage has a small, honest spread and falls off with distance. Double damage is deliberately weaker than in the original — even boosted, a Railgun won't take down a medium tank in one shot. After respawn the tank blinks and is invulnerable for a few seconds, making spawn-camping impossible. The top of the scoreboard goes to whoever drives better, shoots straighter, and picks the moment smarter — not to whoever paid longer.
</Accordion>
