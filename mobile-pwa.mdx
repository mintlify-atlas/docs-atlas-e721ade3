The mobile version of SkillTanki installs straight from the browser as a Progressive Web App: open the link on your phone, tap **Add to Home Screen**, and SkillTanki gets its own icon and runs full-screen like a native app — no store account, no download queue, no waiting.

## Installing the PWA

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the mobile link on your phone">
    Navigate to [https://mobile.skilltanki.workers.dev/](https://mobile.skilltanki.workers.dev/) in your mobile browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap the browser menu">
    - **Safari (iOS):** tap the share icon → **Add to Home Screen**
    - **Chrome / Android:** tap the menu (⋮) → **Install app** or **Add to Home Screen**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the install">
    Confirm when prompted — SkillTanki appears on your home screen with its own icon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the icon and play">
    Tap the icon — the game launches full-screen, just like a native app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  No Google Play, no App Store account, no regional restrictions — the game does not depend on store availability in your country.
</Note>

## Touch controls

All controls appear directly on screen and are designed for one-thumb or two-thumb play:

- **Drive joystick** — left side of the screen; drag to steer and move
- **Fire button** — right side; tap to shoot
- **Turret rotation buttons** — rotate the turret left or right independently of the hull
- **Supplies bar** — equivalent to keyboard keys 1–5; tap any slot to activate a supply

Controls fade automatically when a gamepad is connected, and reappear the moment you touch the screen again.

## Full feature parity

The mobile PWA is the same game as the desktop version — nothing is cut down or simplified:

- **Same garage:** 7 hulls, 9 turrets, all paints — everything unlocked from the first second
- **Same modes:** Deathmatch, Team Deathmatch, Capture the Flag, Assault
- **Solo vs bots** for offline practice, or **multiplayer** with an optional room password
- A **graphics quality switch** keeps the game smooth on older or lower-powered phones

## Multiplayer

The mobile PWA connects to the same rooms as the desktop browser version:

- Public rooms appear in the shared battle list — anyone can join
- **Password-protected rooms** work identically to desktop: the password never shows in the URL or the room list — share the room name and password directly with the people you want to play with

<Tip>
  A ten-minute lunch-break session is a valid scenario — no account means nothing to lose if you close the app.
</Tip>
