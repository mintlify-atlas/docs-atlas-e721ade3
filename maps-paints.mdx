SkillTanki ships with 91 battlefields covering both summer and winter variants of the original classic collection. The cozy "box" maps, the multi-level ramp arenas, the community favourites — all preserved. The map format from the 2010 original is kept alive, and a full map editor ships with the game, so maps lost when the original moved on can be rebuilt from memory and screenshots, and entirely new ones can be created from scratch.

## Maps

The collection breaks down as follows:

- **53 summer maps** — the full range of the classic layout library: open arenas, tight box maps, ramp-heavy multi-tier battlefields, and themed environments.
- **38 winter variants** — the same walls, ramps, geometry, and cover positions as their summer counterparts, reskinned in snow. The layout knowledge transfers directly; only the visual atmosphere changes.

Any map — summer or winter — can be opened in **Explore Map mode**, which lets you drive around the entire battlefield without firing a single shot. No enemies, no timers, no score. It is the cleanest way to study cover positions, ramp trajectories, and flag route options before entering a competitive match.

The map format itself is a first-class feature. The built-in map editor lets you:

- Rebuild maps that were lost when the original game format changed, using screenshots or memory as reference.
- Create entirely new maps from scratch and share them.
- Open and modify any existing map in the collection.

<Note>
  Use Explore Map mode on any battlefield to learn cover positions before entering a competitive match.
</Note>

## Paints

101 purely cosmetic paint schemes are available for your tank — none of them affect armour, speed, or any gameplay statistic whatsoever. Every paint is free and unlocked from the first session.

The range spans plain solid colours at one end and named signature skins at the other. A selection of the paints available:

`red` `blue` `green` `black` `white` `orange` `acid` `amilia` `anubis` `apple` `arab` `barkhan` `besthelper` `blink` `blizzard` `blueray` `carbon` `champion` `clay` `creator` `digital` `dirt` `dragon` `drug` `electra` `euro2012` `flame` `flash` `flora` `flow` … and 71 more.

In team battles — Team Deathmatch, Capture the Flag, and Assault — the classic **red** and **blue** team colours are still used to distinguish sides on screen, regardless of which personal paint each player has selected. You will always be able to tell friend from foe.

<Tip>
  Paint selection is purely personal — pick whatever looks good to you. It has zero effect on battle performance.
</Tip>
