SkillTanki is played entirely from the keyboard — no mouse is needed at any point during a battle. The camera always stays behind the hull, tracking it automatically as you drive, so your focus stays on movement and aim rather than on camera management. Every control is a single key or a key held down; there are no modifier combos to memorise.

## Key reference

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| `WASD` / `↑↓←→` | Drive (forward, back, turn left, turn right) |
| `Z` | Turn turret left |
| `X` | Turn turret right |
| `C` | Centre turret (snap back to forward) |
| `Space` | Fire |
| `Q` | Tilt camera left |
| `E` | Tilt camera right |
| `1` | Supply — Repair kit |
| `2` | Supply — Double armour |
| `3` | Supply — Double damage |
| `4` | Supply — Nitro |
| `5` | Supply — Nuclear energy |
| `0` (hold) | Self-destruct |
| `Tab` | Show / hide scoreboard |

## Supply keys

Each number key from `1` to `5` activates one of the five supplies. All supplies are free, unlimited, and subject only to a per-supply cooldown that is identical for every player.

| Key | Supply | Effect |
|-----|--------|--------|
| `1` | **Repair kit** | Instantly repairs the tank to full health. |
| `2` | **Double armour** | Halves all incoming damage for a short duration. |
| `3` | **Double damage** | Strengthens outgoing shots for a short duration. Note: this is tuned weaker than in the original — even boosted, a Railgun will not one-shot a medium tank. |
| `4` | **Nitro** | Gives a noticeable boost to speed and acceleration. Stronger than in the original and available to all — speed is a positioning skill, not a privilege. |
| `5` | **Nuclear energy** | Instantly readies the gun and speeds up its reload for the next shots. |

Supplies are purely tactical tools. Use them at the right moment and they turn a fight; hoard them and they expire unused.

## Turret control

The turret rotates independently of the hull, which lets you fire sideways or backwards while continuing to drive.

- **`Z`** rotates the turret to the left at a fixed speed.
- **`X`** rotates the turret to the right at the same speed.
- **`C`** snaps the turret instantly back to the forward position — useful after a side-shot when you need to face the direction you're driving without hunting for centre manually.

Turret position is preserved when you stop rotating, so you can pre-angle before rounding a corner and fire as soon as an enemy comes into view.

<Tip>
  The camera follows the hull automatically — use `Q` / `E` only when you want to peek around corners without turning the tank.
</Tip>

## Mobile controls

On mobile devices the keyboard layout is replaced by on-screen controls: a virtual joystick in the bottom-left corner for driving, a Fire button on the right, and dedicated buttons for turret rotation and each supply slot. The layout mirrors the keyboard scheme so the transition between platforms is straightforward.

For full details on the mobile experience — including PWA installation and gamepad support — see the [Mobile & PWA guide](/mobile-pwa).
