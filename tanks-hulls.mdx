SkillTanki keeps the classic line-up intact, from the featherweight Wasp all the way up to the lumbering Mammoth. Every hull from the 2010 original is here — and every one of them is available from your very first session, with no farming, no unlock grind, and no rank gate.

## Hull comparison

| Hull | Character | Best for |
|------|-----------|----------|
| **Wasp** | The lightest and fastest hull in the game — a glass feather that lives entirely by manoeuvre. Fragile under sustained fire, devastating when moving. | Aggressive flanking, hit-and-run ambushes, CTF flag carrying |
| **Hornet** | A step sturdier and more solid than the Wasp, yet still very nimble. The extra armour makes brief exchanges survivable without giving up much speed. | Hit-and-run, fast CTF/Assault flag runs, scouting |
| **Hunter** ⭐ | The golden mean: speed, armour, and weight all held in balance. The textbook "first tank" — nothing extreme in any direction. | Any mode; the best hull for learning the game |
| **Viking** | Wide, stable, and hard to flip. The broad chassis sits flat on rough ground and absorbs bumps without losing aim. A relaxed all-rounder. | Versatile play across all modes; rough-terrain maps |
| **Dictator** | A tall hull with the turret mounted high. That elevation means it can see — and shoot — over cover that hides shorter tanks, but it is equally visible to everyone else. | Maps where height and line-of-sight matter |
| **Titan** | Heavy and unhurried. Soaks up considerably more damage than mid-range hulls before going down. Slower to accelerate and turn. | Anchoring a defence in TDM; absorbing punishment as Isida heals |
| **Mammoth** | The slowest and the toughest. A fortress on tracks — almost impossible to destroy quickly, but equally impossible to reposition quickly. | Front-line tank role in TDM; Isida-supported anchor |

<Info>
  Hunter is the textbook starter — balanced stats make every other hull easier to appreciate once you know what you're comparing.
</Info>

## Choosing your hull

The right hull depends on how you like to fight, not on what mode you're playing:

**Wasp and Hornet** shine in roles that reward speed over staying power. In CTF and Assault, a fast flag carrier who uses Nitro (supply key `4`) is far harder to intercept than a slow one. Both hulls also excel at aggressive flanking in Deathmatch — dart in, fire, dart out before the enemy can respond.

**Hunter and Viking** are versatile enough for any mode. Hunter's balanced stats mean you are rarely at a severe disadvantage in any engagement. Viking's stability on rough ground makes it forgiving on maps with ramps, bumps, and uneven terrain where lighter hulls bounce off course.

**Dictator** pays off specifically on maps where elevation changes the engagement — if you can shoot over a wall that blocks your enemy's return fire, the tall profile becomes an asset rather than a liability.

**Titan and Mammoth** are anchor hulls. Pair either with an Isida-wielding teammate and you become a very hard objective to remove from a key position. In Team Deathmatch, a Titan or Mammoth at a chokepoint while allies pick off flanking enemies is a proven formation.

<Note>
  There is no progression ladder. All hulls are available from your first session.
</Note>
